🧭 EduCoach (all)

1. Show the bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B) and drag the button onto it.
2. Open any math sim (math_1_2 … math_1_13).
3. Click the EduCoach bookmark. A bar appears at the bottom; the right control glows (amber = tap this, green = submit, blue = type here). It speaks one short line per round.

Covers: compare, rounding, ratio, comma-grouping, patterns (incl. squares), speed, and place-value build. Combinatorial sims (arrange/cards) get a strategy line only. Reload the page to remove it; EduCoach.stop() also stops it.
